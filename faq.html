❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Steem Games?

Steem Games is a directory for discovering games built on the Steem blockchain. You can explore game information, compare activity points, and find links to play games.

Do I need to log in to browse games?

No. The game directory and its information are public. Log in only when an account action, such as voting, requires it.

What is D2E?

D2E is the Do2Earn reward token used in the Steem Games ecosystem. Your available D2E balance is shown in the header after you log in.

How do I log in?

You can log in with Steem Keychain or your Steem Posting Key. Posting Key is the default method. The key is verified in the browser and is not saved by this site.

Why is my D2E balance not showing?

Refresh the page and check your connection. If the balance still does not appear, the account balance service may be temporarily unavailable.

How are game points calculated?

Points are based on game activity in the Steem ecosystem. The directory displays rounded point totals for easier comparison.
